Codes used per agent

Tickets per code, with each agent's calls and orders. “Booked (logged)” is what the agent wrote in the call list.

Calls made — every call attempt (1st, 2nd and 3rd) in the lapsed-customer call list, on the date the agent wrote, credited to the initials after the date. Calls with no readable date count in Campaign to date only.
Sales — Megatix orders with a FRIEND (or THERAPY) discount code on any show, each counted under that show's city tab: paid web checkouts only (resales and $0 orders left out) on Melbourne-time days, as on the Sales Desk. This is the same count as the Call centre line on the Sales Desk (a FRIEND sale on a show without call-centre calls yet, such as Adelaide on 15 Oct, still counts). Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
Sales per agent — each order is matched to the call list by email, then phone, and credited to the agent who last called that customer on or before the day they bought. Not matched = the code was used by someone who isn't on the call list (for example a friend they shared it with). No initials = calls logged without initials.
Attributed sales (assists) — a paid web order without a FRIEND code, for the same city's show, by someone on that city's call list, made 0–14 days after their last call attempt (answered or not), matched by email then phone. Strong = an agent spoke to them (Interested, Booked or Call Back Requested) and they bought within 7 days; Likely = bought 0–3 days after a call attempt (usually a missed call, then an SMS); Possible = bought 4–14 days after a call attempt. Credited to the agent on the last call attempt before they bought (one with a positive outcome preferred). Resales, comps, $0 orders and VIP upgrades are left out, and so are customers logged "already has tickets" on a call that day. An assist shows a link between a call and a purchase, not proof the call caused it, so it is kept separate from FRIEND sales and the Sales Desk's Call centre line.
Refreshed each weekday morning by the daily sales run.
